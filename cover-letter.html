The background behind my CVs.

Cheri Favel · MEng Chemical Engineering · Abu Dhabi, UAE

Dear Hiring Manager,

I am an MEng-qualified Chemical Engineer based in Abu Dhabi, with experience across process engineering, experimental research, scientific communication, marine operations and digital content. Process Engineering & R&D remains my core career direction. I am also open to roles where my analytical, organisational and communication skills meet a clear business need.

At Free Radical Process Design, I contributed to laboratory and pilot-scale investigations in hydrometallurgy, electrochemical metal recovery and mine-water treatment. My work included experimental programmes, process calculations, mass and energy balances, flow diagrams, equipment specifications and technical reports. At the University of Pretoria, my postgraduate research investigated nitric-acid leaching of phlogopite, combining experimental design, kinetic modelling and materials characterisation. Research from this work was published in Clays and Clay Minerals.

Following my role at Free Radical Process Design, I took time for family responsibilities after a bereavement, a period that also involved relocation. My later transition from CACTUS Communications to vessel work involved relocating from South Africa to the United States. These circumstances provide context for the time between those roles.

My subsequent work broadened how I apply my technical foundation. Scientific manuscript editing at CACTUS Communications strengthened my ability to assess complex information and communicate it precisely. Working aboard Research Vessel ROSA added hands-on experience in guest service, vessel support, procurement, inventory and crew coordination. My work with Cresco Consulting has focused on branding, website and marketing content, alongside training and exposure to UAE business-setup processes. Although these roles were outside a dedicated process-engineering position, they developed practical strengths I can bring into a technical environment or apply in other functions.

The different CVs on my website reflect these connections. Research, technical editing and AI-assisted content workflows are relevant to digital and content roles; documentation, Excel, procurement and scheduling support administration and operations. University teaching, research mentoring and online English tutoring underpin my interest in education. Yacht stewardessing and guest-service experience provide a direct foundation for hospitality roles. My AI experience is practical use of tools for professional work; my engineering qualification is in chemical engineering.

My openness to different roles reflects this experience and a willingness to contribute where there is a good fit. I would approach each position on its own responsibilities, with respect for the expertise it requires. In engineering, I would bring my research and process-development foundation, refresh relevant knowledge and learn the organisation's methods. In another function, I would focus on the experience that makes me useful in that role. I value clear expectations, dependable work and the opportunity to build expertise within a team.

I hold a UAE Resident Visa and am available immediately. I would welcome a conversation about your needs, my career progression and where my experience could contribute. Thank you for considering my profile.
